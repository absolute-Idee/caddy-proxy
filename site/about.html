About

I'm Mark, a software developer by day. In the evenings and on weekends I'm usually somewhere with a camera that is older than I am.

I mostly shoot 35mm black and white, developed at home in a kitchen that was never designed for it. Colour goes to a lab. Scanning I do myself, which is how the scripts started.

This site is a plain set of HTML files. No tracking, no comments, no newsletter. If something here was useful, or wrong, I'd like to hear about it.

Gear, for the curious

An Olympus OM-2n with a 50mm f/1.8, a Pentax MX that I keep meaning to sell, and a mirrorless body with a macro lens on a copy stand for digitising.
